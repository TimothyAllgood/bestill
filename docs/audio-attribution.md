# Built-in audio provenance
Public-domain hymn melodies, not modern recordings or arrangements, used in newly synthesized instrumental settings.
- Amazing Grace / NEW BRITAIN: traditional 19th-century melody. https://hymnary.org/tune/new_britain
- Doxology / OLD HUNDREDTH: Genevan Psalter (1551), attributed to Louis Bourgeois. https://hymnary.org/tune/old_hundredth_bourgeois
- Abide with Me / EVENTIDE: William Henry Monk (1861). https://hymnary.org/tune/eventide_monk
- Morning Light and Quiet Waters: original ambient note/chord sequences for this project.
No third-party samples, lyrics, sheet-music images or commercial recordings distributed. Offline Web Audio synthesis with local looping, new voicing and phrasing. These are synthesized instrumental sketches rather than professional piano recordings; listening and melody review on real hardware remain outstanding.

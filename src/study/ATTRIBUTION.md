# Included commentary
Matthew Henry's Concise Commentary on the Whole Bible (historical text, public domain; completed in part by continuators). Source edition: https://github.com/lyteword/mhenry-concise/tree/a6203191a402166dc3be5286aaf66e4ce3c3f4aa, CC0 1.0 (license included). CrossWire independently identifies the concise commentary as public domain: https://crosswire.org/sword/modules/ModInfo.jsp?modName=MHCC. The app includes complete matching verse-range sections for its twenty-six curated passages, copied from pinned Markdown files with heading and outer whitespace removed. No AI translation, summary or rewriting. Each entry includes upstream path and blob hash. Historical Protestant devotional interpretation, not Scripture or modern scholarly consensus. ESV remains display-only.
